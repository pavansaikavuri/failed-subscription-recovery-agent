Key Finding
Compliance-guarded recovery (agent_rules) overtakes unconstrained retrying (naive_rules) at an exact penalty of ₹888.67 per violation. Above ₹1,351.22, even an unconstrained profit-maximizing Oracle with full model knowledge completely eliminates all violations, verifying that regulatory penalties enforce optimal compliant behavior.
Expected-Value Gating on Human Escalation
Theoretical expected gain from the EV gate is ₹176.40. Realised gain across 200 seeds is ₹145.01 — below theory, because human escalation happened to over-perform its 35% prior in these seeds (38% realised). The measured benefit is therefore conservative, not flattered by sampling.

Paired across 200 identical seeds, the EV gate improves agent_rules net by ₹145.01 ± ₹18.36. The effect is unambiguous and small: 0.15% of the ₹98,952 book. Its significance is structural rather than monetary — escalating a ₹299 payment at a ₹150 support cost is wrong regardless of how it samples, and the gate removes that class of decision.

The Compliance Pricing Curve

Three programmatically derived penalty thresholds establish the economic boundaries of autonomous subscription recovery on this batch:

Penalty Threshold Economic Event Market & Behavioral Meaning
₹62.08 Cheapest violation stops paying Below ₹62.08, no violation is deterred; every unlawful retry yields higher net expected margin than its best compliant alternative. At ₹62.08, pay_Ex66fGhIjKlM65 (₹899, insufficient funds) ceases to be profitable to retry unlawfully and switches to compliant notice reissuance.
₹888.67 Compliant agent overtakes naive rulebook At ₹888.67, agent_rules strictly overtakes naive_rules net of compliance risk. naive_rules is unchanged; agent_rules rose by the EV gate, shrinking the gross performance gap so the crossing arrives earlier. Between ₹888.67 and ₹1,351.22, compliance is the superior system-wide policy while individual high-value violations remain locally profitable.
